CADAC

CADAC is a fictitious visual identity for an imagined Contemporary Architectural Center based in Switzerland. The system is structured around the principle of paper folding, translating architectural logic into a graphic language of sharp planes and shifting geometries. Rather than relying on a fixed logo, the identity operates as a generative system, producing evolving compositions through a defined folding structure. A custom digital tool was developed in JavaScript to generate and control the folding effect across applications.
