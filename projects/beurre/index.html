Beurre

Beurre is a creative coding project developed during a one-week workshop centered on the theme of satisfaction. The objective was to design a single interaction focused entirely on sensory precision. What could be more satisfying than cutting a perfectly smooth slice of butter? Nothing.

Collaboration: Mario Van Rickenbach, Sébatien Matos
